Compression

Every chapter, the model rewrites the whole story-so-far inside a word budget rather than appending to it. Scene memory is separate: scene-shaped, bounded, and paired with at most one recent prose sample. Appending is not compression — it just defers the problem until the context breaks. This is the part that gets better as models get better.
